1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to Roseluna (the “App”), a tarot, dream and daily-fortune application published by the Roseluna team (“we”, “us”, “our”). For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, the Roseluna team is the data controller of the personal information described in this policy.

Roseluna has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not operate a user database, and we do not associate the data described below with any persistent user identity.

This policy is governed by the laws applicable to the publisher's place of establishment, without prejudice to any mandatory consumer protections in your country or state of residence.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide voluntarily

Birth profile (used to generate your readings)

To personalise your daily fortune and tarot readings, the App processes the birth information you enter: date of birth, time of birth, and birth city. When the App generates an AI-powered reading, the relevant parts of this information are sent to a large-language-model (LLM) provider listed in Section 4 to produce the text. Your profile itself is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to us.

Dream journal and tarot questions

When you ask a tarot question, submit a dream for interpretation, or use the dream-number conversion, the text you type is sent to the LLM provider to generate your reading. Please do not include information in these texts that you would not want a third-party AI service to process.

Feedback

When you submit feedback through Settings → Feedback, we receive only the fields described below. Providing contact details is entirely optional.

FieldPurpose
Feedback typeCategorising your report (problem or suggestion)
Message contentUnderstanding and fixing the issue
Contact (optional)Replying to you only if you ask us to
Platform & app versionReproducing the issue on the right build
TimestampOrdering and following up on reports
Please note Never include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in a feedback message.

2.2 No tracking, no ads, no analytics SDKs

Roseluna contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect advertising identifiers (AAID/IDFA), we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not build behavioural profiles. The App calls our LLM providers and the feedback endpoint directly; nothing else is transmitted.

2.3 Data that stays on your device

The following is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server:

  • Your birth profile (date, time, birth city)
  • Your dream journal entries and mood records
  • Tarot reading history and lucky-colour / lucky-time records
  • App preferences (language, notification settings)

Uninstalling the App deletes all of the above from your device.

3. How we use your information

We use the information described above only for the following purposes. Where the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we rely on the legal bases indicated:

PurposeLegal basis (GDPR)
Generate your daily fortune, tarot and dream readings with an LLM providerConsent (you choose when to generate a reading)
Answer and act on the feedback you send usConsent, and legitimate interests in supporting our users
Deliver the daily-fortune notification you opted intoConsent

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your birth data, dreams or questions for advertising, profiling or model training by us. We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Device permissions

The App requests the following device permissions, only when you actively use the corresponding feature. It never accesses your location or photos in the background.

  • Location — only when you tap “use my location” while setting your birth city, to fill in the city name. Used once, in the foreground only.
  • Notifications — only if you opt in, to deliver the daily-fortune reminder. You can turn it off any time in Settings.
  • Photos / media library — to save a share card to your gallery when you explicitly tap save.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings; the related features will simply stop working.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: your birth profile, dream journal and history persist until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • Feedback you sent: retained only as long as needed to handle your report and improve the App, after which it is deleted.
  • LLM prompts: processed by the LLM providers under their own retention policies; we do not store your prompts or readings on any server.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we handle, including restricting access to feedback data to the small team responsible for support. Because your core data never leaves your device, it also benefits from your device's own security (screen lock, disk encryption). No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

In the unlikely event of a personal data breach that affects you, we will notify affected users and, where required, the relevant data protection authorities, in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

Roseluna is a general-audience entertainment app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age set by the laws of your jurisdiction, which may be up to 16 in some EU member states). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your rights

9.1 EEA and UK (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9.2 California (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know, delete, and correct personal information. We do not sell or “share” personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not collect “sensitive personal information” as defined by the CCPA. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

9.3 Other regions

Residents of other US states with comprehensive privacy laws (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and Utah) have similar rights, including access, deletion, correction and portability. Where local law grants you comparable rights, we will honour them through the same channels.

9.4 How to exercise your rights

Because Roseluna has no accounts, we hold no profile that identifies you. Most of your data lives on your device and can be deleted directly by uninstalling the App. For feedback data or anything else, contact us via Section 12; we aim to respond within the period required by applicable law (and in any event within 7 days where feasible).

10. International data transfers

Feedback you submit is stored on a server located in Singapore. Text you submit for readings is processed by our LLM providers, whose servers may be located in other countries (including the United States and mainland China, depending on the provider used). Where required, these transfers rely on adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and, where the change is material, notify you inside the App. Continuing to use Roseluna after a change means you accept the updated policy.
