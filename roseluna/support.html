About the app

Roseluna is your daily fortune companion — daily tarot card readings from the full Major Arcana, Thai dream interpretation with lucky numbers, a personal daily fortune based on your birth date and today's moon phase, Buddhist holy days (Wan Phra) with a merit check-in, and a private mood & spiritual journal.

Your journal and dreams stay yours. An account (email sign-in) is only needed to sync your reading history and journal.

Frequently asked questions · 常见问题 · คำถามที่พบบ่อย

Why does my daily fortune change every day? / 为什么每日运势每天都会变？ Your fortune is calculated from your birth date, birth region and today's moon phase, so it is refreshed once a day, around midnight local time. 每日运势基于出生日期、出生地区与当日月相计算，每天更新一次。
I can't sign in / I forgot my password. / 无法登录或忘记密码？ On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password" and we will email you a reset link. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder and try again in a few minutes. 在登录页点击"忘记密码"，我们会发送重置邮件；如未收到请检查垃圾邮件文件夹。
Can I delete my account and data? / 可以删除账号和数据吗？ Yes. Contact us by email or GitHub issue and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data. 可以。通过邮件或 GitHub issue 联系我们，我们将彻底删除你的账号与全部关联数据。
Are the readings real advice? / 占卜结果是真实建议吗？ Roseluna is for entertainment and self-reflection only. Readings follow Thai tarot and dream-oracle traditions and are not a substitute for professional, medical, legal or financial advice. 本应用仅供娱乐与自我反思，解读内容依据泰式塔罗与传统梦书，不构成任何专业建议。
