1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to AquaKeeper (the “App”), an aquarium management application published by the AquaKeeper team (“we”, “us”, “our”). For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, the AquaKeeper team is the data controller of the personal information described in this policy.

This policy does not apply to third-party services integrated into the App (such as Google Firebase or Google AdMob). Those services process data under their own privacy policies, which are linked in Section 4.

This policy is governed by the laws applicable to the publisher's place of establishment, without prejudice to any mandatory consumer protections in your country or state of residence.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide voluntarily

When you submit feedback through Settings → Feedback, we receive only the fields described below. Providing contact details is entirely optional.

Please note Never include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in a feedback message.

2.2 Information collected automatically

To keep the App stable and to show ads, the following third-party services collect certain data automatically when you use AquaKeeper:

ServiceWhat it collects
Firebase Analytics In-app events and feature usage, app version, device model, OS version, region (derived from IP)
Firebase Crashlytics Crash stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, free storage / memory state at the time of the crash
Firebase Remote Config App instance identifier and app version, used to deliver remote feature switches
Google AdMob Advertising identifier (AAID on Android / IDFA on iOS), ad requests, ad interactions, and your consent choices
App store update check On iOS we query Apple's public lookup API; on Android we use Google Play's in-app update API. Both only receive the App's bundle identifier.

2.3 Device permissions

The App requests the following device permissions, only when you actively use the corresponding feature. It never accesses the camera or your photos in the background.

  • Camera — to take photos of your aquarium when you choose the camera option.
  • Photos / media library — to import photos you select, and to save images you explicitly export.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings; the related features will simply stop working.

2.4 Data that stays on your device

The following is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server:

  • Aquarium profiles (tank names, sizes, livestock notes)
  • Water test records and parameter history
  • Photos you take or import from your gallery
  • Maintenance tasks, reminders and calculation history
  • App preferences (language, dark mode, units)
  • Your purchase entitlement status

Exporting your data creates a ZIP file on your device that you share through your device's own share sheet. We do not receive a copy.

5. Advertising and your choices

AquaKeeper uses Google AdMob to display ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any personalised ad is loaded. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time.

On iOS, AquaKeeper does not request tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. As a result, Google serves non-personalised ads on iOS.

You can also control advertising at the operating-system level:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, to turn off personalised ads.
  • Google account: visit My Ad Center to control how ads are personalised across Google services.

If you are a California resident, please also see Section 9 for your opt-out rights regarding the “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: your aquarium data persists until you delete it. Use Settings → Data → Clear all data, or simply uninstall the App.
  • Feedback you sent: retained only as long as needed to handle your report and improve the App, after which it is deleted.
  • Analytics and crash data: retained by Google Firebase according to the retention period configured in our Firebase project, after which it is automatically deleted.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we handle, including restricting access to feedback data to the small team responsible for support. Because your core data never leaves your device, it also benefits from your device's own security (screen lock, disk encryption). No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

In the unlikely event of a personal data breach that affects you, we will notify affected users and, where required, the relevant data protection authorities, in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

AquaKeeper is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age set by the laws of your jurisdiction, which may be up to 16 in some EU member states). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through a feedback message, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your rights

9.1 EEA and UK (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9.2 California (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell personal information for money. However, the advertising identifier collected by Google AdMob may constitute “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law. You can opt out at any time using the controls in Section 5 (system advertising settings or My Ad Center). We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights. We do not collect “sensitive personal information” as defined by the CCPA.

9.3 Other US states and other regions

Residents of other US states with comprehensive privacy laws (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and Utah) have similar rights, including access, deletion, correction and portability. Where local law grants you comparable rights, we will honour them through the same channels.

9.4 How to exercise your rights

Because AquaKeeper has no accounts, we hold no profile that identifies you. Most requests can be resolved directly on your device by exporting or clearing your data. For anything else, email us at support@aquakeeper.app; we aim to respond within the period required by applicable law (and in any event within 7 days where feasible).

10. International data transfers

Feedback you submit is stored on a server located in Singapore. Analytics, crash and advertising data are processed by Google, which may transfer and store data in the United States and other countries. Where required, these transfers rely on adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

11. Do Not Track

“Do Not Track” (DNT) is a browser setting; AquaKeeper is a native app, not a website, so DNT signals do not apply. Advertising choices are controlled through the settings described in Section 5.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and, where the change is material, notify you inside the App. Continuing to use AquaKeeper after a change means you accept the updated policy.
