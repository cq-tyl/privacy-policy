1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to Roseluna (the “App”), a tarot, dream and daily-fortune application published by the Roseluna team (“we”, “us”, “our”). For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, the Roseluna team is the data controller of the personal information described in this policy.

The App offers an optional account (email sign-up and sign-in powered by Google Firebase Authentication). Creating an account is not required to use the App, but it enables cross-device cloud sync of your birth profile and your content.

This policy is governed by the laws applicable to the publisher's place of establishment, without prejudice to any mandatory consumer protections in your country or state of residence.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information

If you create an account, we collect your email address through Google Firebase Authentication. This is required for registration, sign-in and password reset. You can also request a verification email to confirm your address.

2.2 Birth profile (cloud-synced for logged-in users)

To personalise your daily fortune and tarot readings, the App processes the birth information you enter: date of birth, time of birth, and birth city. If you are signed in, this profile is synced to your private cloud document so your readings work across devices. When the App generates an AI-powered reading, the relevant parts of this information are sent to an LLM provider listed in Section 5 to produce the text. If you are not signed in, your profile stays only on your device.

2.3 Your content (cloud-synced for logged-in users)

If you are signed in, the content you create in the App is cloud-synced and stored in a single Firestore document reserved for your account: tarot reading logs (including the question text you type), partner compatibility logs, dream diary entries, and mood history. If you are not signed in, this content stays only on your device. Please do not include information in these texts that you would not want a third-party AI service to process.

2.4 Analytics and crash data

We use Google Firebase Analytics to record app-open, sign-in and sign-up events, and Google Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash logs. Crash reports are associated with an account identifier to help us diagnose issues affecting you.

2.5 Location (one-time, on-device only)

If you grant permission, the App performs a single, foreground-only location lookup to suggest the city nearest to you when you set up your birth profile. The coordinates are used on your device only — they are never stored, never uploaded, and the App never tracks your location in the background.

2.6 Feedback

When you submit feedback through Settings → Feedback, we receive the fields described below. Providing contact details is entirely optional.

Please note Never include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in a feedback message.

2.7 AI processing

When you request an interpretation (tarot, dream, daily fortune, or compatibility), the App sends the relevant prompt — which may include your birth data and your question — to our AI service providers, DeepSeek and Zhipu AI, solely to generate your reading.

2.8 What we do NOT collect

The App contains no advertising SDKs and no in-app purchases. We do not collect advertising identifiers for ad targeting, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not collect contacts, photos, or precise location history.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on your device and, for logged-in users, on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics), hosted by Google LLC. All data transmitted between the App and our servers and third-party services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we handle, including restricting access to feedback data to the small team responsible for support. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

In the unlikely event of a personal data breach that affects you, we will notify affected users and, where required, the relevant data protection authorities, in accordance with applicable law.

6. Device permissions

The App requests the following device permissions, only when you actively use the corresponding feature. It never accesses your location or photos in the background.

  • Location — only when you tap “use my location” while setting your birth city, to fill in the city name. Used once, in the foreground only.
  • Notifications — only if you opt in, to deliver the daily-fortune reminder. You can turn it off any time in Settings.
  • Photos / media library — to save a share card to your gallery when you explicitly tap save.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings; the related features will simply stop working.

7. Delete your account

You can delete your account from within the App: Settings → Account → Delete Account (confirm twice when prompted).

Deleting your account removes your Firebase Authentication account and erases the associated cloud data document, including your birth profile, tarot and compatibility logs, dream diary, and mood history. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the App, you may request account deletion by emailing us at Section 13; we will verify your request and delete the associated cloud data.

8. Data retention

  • On your device: your birth profile, dream journal and history persist until you delete them or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data.
  • In the cloud (logged-in users): your synced data is retained until you delete your account (see Section 7) or request deletion.
  • Feedback you sent: retained only as long as needed to handle your report and improve the App, after which it is deleted.
  • Analytics data: retained in accordance with Firebase's standard retention settings.
  • LLM prompts: processed by the LLM providers under their own retention policies.

9. Children's privacy

Roseluna is a general-audience entertainment app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age set by the laws of your jurisdiction, which may be up to 16 in some EU member states). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Your rights

10.1 EEA and UK (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10.2 California (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know, delete, and correct personal information. We do not sell or “share” personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not collect “sensitive personal information” as defined by the CCPA. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

10.3 Other regions

Residents of other US states with comprehensive privacy laws (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and Utah) have similar rights, including access, deletion, correction and portability. Where local law grants you comparable rights, we will honour them through the same channels.

10.4 How to exercise your rights

You can view and edit your birth profile and your content in the App at any time, and you can delete your account (and its cloud data) directly in the App via Section 7. For feedback data or anything else, contact us via Section 13; we aim to respond within the period required by applicable law (and in any event within 7 days where feasible).

11. International data transfers

Cloud data of logged-in users is hosted by Google Firebase, whose infrastructure is distributed globally (including the United States). Feedback you submit is stored on a server located in Singapore. Text you submit for readings is processed by our LLM providers, whose servers may be located in other countries (including the United States and mainland China, depending on the provider used). Where required, these transfers rely on adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses or other lawful transfer mechanisms.
