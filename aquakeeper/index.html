At a glance 要点速览

  • Your aquarium data (tanks, water tests, photos, tasks) stays on your device — we never upload or sync it.
  • No account, no sign-up, no email address required to use the app.
  • We do use Google Firebase (analytics & crash reports) and Google AdMob (ads), which collect device and usage identifiers.
  • The only content you send to our own server is a feedback message you choose to submit.
  • You can export or permanently delete all local data at any time from Settings → Data.
  • 你的鱼缸数据（鱼缸、水质检测、照片、维护任务）仅保存在你的设备上，我们不会上传或同步。
  • 无需注册账号，也不用提供邮箱即可使用全部核心功能。
  • 我们确实使用了 Google Firebase（分析与崩溃报告）和 Google AdMob（广告），它们会收集设备与使用情况标识符。
  • 你主动发送到我们自有服务器的内容，仅限你自愿提交的意见反馈。
  • 你可以随时在「我的 → 数据」中导出或彻底清除全部本地数据。

To keep the app stable and to show ads, the following third-party services collect certain data automatically when you use AquaKeeper:

为了维持应用稳定以及展示广告，以下第三方服务会在你使用 AquaKeeper 时自动收集部分数据：

1.3 Data that stays on your device 1.3 仅保存在你设备上的数据

The following is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server:

以下内容仅保存在你的设备本地，绝不会传输给我们或任何服务器：

  • Aquarium profiles (tank names, sizes, livestock notes)
  • Water test records and parameter history
  • Photos you take or import from your gallery
  • Maintenance tasks, reminders and calculation history
  • App preferences (language, dark mode, units)
  • Your purchase entitlement status
  • 鱼缸档案（鱼缸名称、尺寸、生物备注）
  • 水质检测记录与参数历史
  • 你拍摄或从相册导入的照片
  • 维护任务、提醒与换算历史
  • 应用偏好设置（语言、深色模式、单位）
  • 你的购买权益状态

Exporting your data creates a ZIP file on your device that you share through your device's own share sheet. We do not receive a copy.

导出数据会在你的设备上生成一个 ZIP 文件，并通过系统分享面板发出，我们不会收到副本。

  • To notify you when a new version of the app is available.
  • 回复并处理你提交的意见反馈。
  • 诊断崩溃问题，提升应用稳定性。

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your aquarium data for advertising or profiling.

我们不会出售你的个人信息，也不会将你的鱼缸数据用于广告或用户画像。

4. Advertising and your choices 4. 广告与你的选择

AquaKeeper uses Google AdMob to display ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any personalised ad is loaded. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time.

AquaKeeper 使用 Google AdMob 展示广告。如果你位于欧洲经济区、英国或瑞士，我们会在加载任何个性化广告之前，通过 Google 的用户消息平台（UMP）征求你的同意。你可以随时更改或撤回同意。

You can also control advertising at the operating-system level:

你还可以在操作系统层面控制广告相关设置：

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → reset advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, to disable permission to track; and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising to disable personalised ads.
  • Resetting or limiting your advertising identifier stops it being used for personalised advertising.
  • Android：设置 → Google → 广告 → 重置广告 ID 或退出广告个性化。
  • iOS：设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪，关闭允许跟踪；以及设置 → 隐私与安全性 → Apple 广告，关闭个性化广告。
  • 重置或限制广告标识符后，它将不再被用于个性化广告。

5. Data retention and deletion 5. 数据保留与删除

  • On your device: your aquarium data persists until you delete it. Use Settings → Data → Clear all data, or simply uninstall the app.
  • Feedback you sent: retained only as long as needed to handle your report and improve the app, after which it is deleted.
  • Analytics and crash data: retained by Google Firebase according to the retention period configured in our Firebase project, after which it is automatically deleted.
  • 设备上的数据：你的鱼缸数据会一直保留，直到你主动删除。可通过「我的 → 数据 → 清除所有数据」操作，或直接卸载应用。
  • 你提交的反馈：仅在处理你的反馈和改进应用所必需的期限内保留，之后删除。
  • 分析与崩溃数据：由 Google Firebase 按我们 Firebase 项目中配置的保留期限保存，到期后自动删除。

6. Data security 6. 数据安全

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we handle, including restricting access to feedback data to the small team responsible for support. Because your core data never leaves your device, it also benefits from your device's own security (screen lock, disk encryption). No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

我们采取合理的技术与组织措施来保护所处理的信息，包括将反馈数据的访问权限限制在负责支持的少数人员范围内。由于你的核心数据从不出设备，它同时也受到设备自身安全机制（锁屏、磁盘加密）的保护。互联网上的传输与存储方式无法做到绝对安全，因此我们无法保证绝对的安全性。

7. Children's privacy 7. 儿童隐私

AquaKeeper is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through a feedback message, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

AquaKeeper 是面向普通用户的工具型应用，并非面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会在知情的情况下收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为有儿童通过意见反馈向我们提供了个人信息，请与我们联系，我们会立即删除。

8. Your rights 8. 你的权利

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

根据你所在地区的法律，你可能有权访问、更正、删除我们所持有的关于你的个人信息，或获取其副本，有权反对或限制特定的处理活动，并有权随时撤回同意。你也有权向当地数据保护监管机构提出投诉。

Because AquaKeeper has no accounts, we hold no profile that identifies you. Most requests can be resolved directly on your device by exporting or clearing your data. For anything else, email us at the address below.

由于 AquaKeeper 不设账号，我们并不持有能够识别你身份的用户档案。大多数请求都可以直接在设备上通过导出或清除数据来解决。其他情况请发送邮件至下方地址。

9. International data transfers 9. 数据跨境传输

Feedback you submit is stored on a server located in Singapore. Analytics, crash and advertising data are processed by Google, which may transfer and store data in the United States and other countries. These transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

你提交的反馈存储在位于新加坡的服务器上。分析、崩溃与广告数据由 Google 处理，可能会被传输并存储在美国及其他国家。这些传输依赖标准合同条款或其他合法的传输机制。

我们可能会不时更新本政策。更新时，我们会修改页面顶部的「最近更新」日期；若属于重大变更，还会在应用内通知你。变更后继续使用 AquaKeeper，即表示你接受更新后的政策。
