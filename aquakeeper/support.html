Fish tank water quality tracker · 鱼缸管家

About the app

AquaKeeper turns any water test into a clear 0–100 health score, tracks parameters (pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate), logs maintenance with a calendar, and provides 8 aquarium calculators including a water change calculator.

Your aquarium data stays on your device. We do not collect or upload your tank data.

Frequently asked questions · 常见问题

Why does my score drop after a water change? / 为什么换水后分数反而下降？ A large water change can temporarily destabilize parameters. Re-test after 24 hours — the score usually recovers. 大量换水可能暂时扰动水质参数，建议 24 小时后复测。
How do I change my test reminder schedule? / 如何修改检测提醒周期？ Go to the Reminders screen and edit the retest interval per tank. 进入“提醒”页面，按鱼缸单独调整复测周期。
Where is my data stored? / 数据存在哪里？ All tank data is stored locally on your device. Export anytime from Settings. 所有鱼缸数据仅存储在本地，可随时在设置中导出。
Can I use AquaKeeper on multiple devices? / 可以多设备同步吗？ Data sync across devices is planned for a future update. 多设备同步计划在未来版本中提供。
